Project Title: Enhancing Water Sustainability in Los Angeles: Challenges and Solutions

Location Chosen

Los Angeles, CA, United States

Water Sources, Impact Factors, and Usage

Los Angeles relies heavily on a complex network of imported water, drawing from the Colorado River Aqueduct, the State Water Project, and the Los Angeles Aqueduct. This imported supply is supplemented by local groundwater pumping and recycled water. Key impact factors threatening these sources include prolonged multi-year droughts, a diminishing Sierra Nevada snowpack driven by climate change, and severe regional wildfires that strip vegetation and degrade the quality of watershed runoff.
